- Specs are durable: an approved spec is authored directly under `docs/specs/` as `NNNN-<slug>.md` and archived there, never overwritten by the next change, per `spec_method.md`. A spec or ADR number is never reused: a superseded or withdrawn record is marked in place and keeps its number and its file, rather than being deleted. The marking is a `## Status` section added immediately below the record's title, reading `Retired — superseded by <record>` or `Withdrawn — <reason>`; nothing else in the record is edited, so the archive keeps what was actually approved. The Author checks the rule by hand, per `isolated_review.md`. See `spec_method.md`.
